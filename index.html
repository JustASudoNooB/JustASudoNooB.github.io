NIFTY Vol Desk

End-of-day NIFTY options analytics, from NSE’s raw bhavcopy files to a website that shows what option prices imply after every close.

  • Forwards from put-call parity, Black-76 vols and Greeks on every strike; SVI smiles joined into an arbitrage-checked surface with Dupire local vol
  • India VIX rebuilt from the chain with NSE’s own method; the implied distribution behind a fan of ranges and the odds of any level
  • Black, SABR, Merton, Heston, Bates and local vol fitted each day, then priced on exotics to measure model risk; Heston by COS matches the published benchmark to six decimals
  • Straddle and iron-fly backtests with NSE taxes, slippage and SPAN-style margin, picked in-sample and judged out of sample, with P&L split into theta, gamma and vega
  • A strategy builder, a delta-hedging simulator, a structured-note pricer and the twelve standard pricing models explained in plain language

AMRIP indices

nsepipe

An installable Python library that turns NSE’s daily files into a clean, adjusted, point-in-time stock dataset, and keeps a record of every fix.

  • 2025 backfill: 498 files, 695K stock-days and 3,104 stocks, with histories chained across 60 ISIN changes and 55 renames
  • Found that NSE’s previous-close field is not adjusted for corporate actions, so inferred 139 splits and bonuses from price breaks instead; false moves above 25% fell from 133 to 4
  • 20 data-quality checks with source-file lineage caught 10 holiday-dated files carrying stale data and a missed Saturday Budget session
  • DuckDB with SQL views, a point-in-time feature panel with a no-lookahead test, an interactive QC report and CI
